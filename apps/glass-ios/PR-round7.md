# Glass iOS round 7: Live Activity fix and real-device polish

## Summary

- Live Activity: bump to 0.1.1 (build 17, now 18) and run under a new `LensConnectionAttributes` type (`status`, `pulse`) instead of changing `ConnectionActivityAttributes` in place, so a round 5 or 6 widget never has to decode the new state. Launch ends leftover activities of both types. `KeepAlive.update()` starts the activity whenever it is wanted and none is running, not only on resign-active.
- The Lens in the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen is drawn with SwiftUI shapes (`LensGlyph`), shared with the app's mark, instead of an appex asset. It keeps the teal mark and the gentle pulse, stays still with Reduce Motion, and shows no timer.
- Background connection settings say plainly why it is not running: the toggle is off, location is not allowed, Live Activities are off, or the last start request failed (with its error).
- Threads panel on iPhone: one floating Liquid Glass panel with 30-point continuous corners, inset from the screen edges and full height within the safe area. It is laid out in three zones on that one surface: a 44-point header row (close, title, filter and compose) and the search field, each 16 points from the panel's edges; the list, which fades out gradually where it meets them, ending a full row above the footer; and a footer with Settings and Desktop at one height, 16 points from the sides and bottom. Nothing floats over the rows. Rows are transparent; the selection is a soft raised glass capsule (no teal fill, a faint dark tint in light mode so it reads on iPad) with a semibold title. While the panel is open the chat behind it is dimmed and blurred and its toolbar and composer step away, so the panel is the only glass on screen. iPad's sidebar uses the same zones.
- Thread rows: friendly model names (`deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-free` shows as "DeepSeek V4.1 Flash"), no trailing separator, a time instead of repeating "Yesterday". Generated titles skip greetings, retitle from the first real request, and get a number when they would repeat. The thread count line is gone; Jump to group lives in the filter menu.
- Chat header: the chat's background sits behind the status bar and the controls row and fades out below them (`HeaderFade`), with a material blur under the fade and a soft scroll edge, so text scrolls away before it reaches the title instead of running under it. The title falls back to the first real request rather than a generic host title. Jump to latest is a 44-point arrow-only Liquid Glass circle, tinted with the page colour and shadowed so it reads over text and code in both modes, above the composer that exists only while you are scrolled away. Work in progress no longer expands or collapses on its own as a reply streams.
- Replies always render whole: the 5,000-character "Show more" cut for assistant replies is gone. Long tool output still collapses, and its button now says how many lines it holds.
- Streaming pace: `RevealingReply` releases text at a steady rate that follows how fast it has been arriving (it assumes a slow network at first and holds the longest recent pause, forgetting it slowly) and speeds up as the backlog grows, so bursts and pauses from the network read as an even flow; words arrive whole. A reply that is only just starting reveals its first words too, and a row rebuilt mid-stream carries on from where it was instead of showing everything at once.
- Scrolling while streaming: the transcript stays pinned to the bottom with a size-change scroll anchor instead of animated `scrollTo` calls, which could land on a lazy row's estimated position and leave the iPhone transcript blank mid-reply. It settles once more when the finished reply replaces the streamed one.
- Streaming cost: while a reply streams, everything before its last settled paragraph break is parsed once and cached (`MarkdownView.settledBreak`), only the growing tail is parsed per frame, and settled blocks are `Equatable` so they skip re-rendering. The finished reply is parsed whole, once.
- Streaming fade: newly revealed text eases from 15% to full opacity over 0.3 s (ease-out), each chunk's tail a little behind its head, so the newest words read as a soft gradient. One `TextRenderer` per block (`StreamFade`) fades only the newest two blocks; settled glyphs draw untouched. Reduce Motion shortens it to a near-instant crossfade. A reply that ends its turn gets a copy and share row once the turn finishes.

- `apps/glass-ios/scripts/test.sh`: Glass unit and UI tests on iPhone 17 (iOS 26.5).
- Release-path Live Activity states and the round 5 upgrade: Release-built `Glass.app` driven by `LiveHostE2ETests.testKeepAliveLiveActivityStates` and `testUpgradeFromRound5EndsTheOldActivity` against the E2E harness on iPhone 17 Pro Max.
- `testStreamingReplyIsFollowedUntilYouScrollThenResumes`, `testBurstyLongReplyRevealsSteadily`, `testFinishedReplyShowsCopyAndShare` and `testThreadsPanelOverBusyChatShots` on iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPad Pro 13-inch, light and dark.
- `scripts/package-unsigned-ipa.sh round7`: arm64, no `_CodeSignature` or `embedded.mobileprovision` in the app or widget, build 18.
