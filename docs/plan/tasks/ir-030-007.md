# Objective

Validate draft Web IR output against minimum entity and relationship shapes
recorded by the versioned fixture corpus.

# Contract

- Fixture expectations express minimum entity and relationship counts.
- Opaque-region counts are exact because they represent explicit coverage
  boundaries.
- Vocabulary parsing rejects corpus kinds that the draft contract does not
  recognize.
- Expectation validation runs after normal graph validation and reports stable
  machine-readable paths.
- This remains validation-only; it does not promote the draft to a stable IR
  contract or dispatch browser actions.

# Verification

- draft expectation unit tests;
- fixture vocabulary integration test;
- `cargo test --all-targets --locked`;
- `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features --locked -- -D warnings`;
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check`;
- fixture corpus validation.
