# Objective

Generate inspectable candidates from current semantic targets without hidden
selection or stale execution.

# Delivered

Added deterministic candidate generation from scoped semantic regions and
revisioned targets. Role/name/region/route evidence is exposed by category;
negative constraints produce bounded excluded candidates. Exact, high, low,
ambiguous, not-found, stale, and policy-rejected outcomes are classified
explicitly. Visibility/enabled constraints fail closed until actionability
validation can prove them.
