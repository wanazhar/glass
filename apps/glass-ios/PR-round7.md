# Glass iOS round 7: Live Activity fix and real-device polish

## Summary

- Live Activity: bump to 0.1.1 (17) and run under a new `LensConnectionAttributes` type (`status`, `pulse`) instead of changing `ConnectionActivityAttributes` in place, so a round 5 or 6 widget never has to decode the new state. Launch ends leftover activities of both types. `KeepAlive.update()` starts the activity whenever it is wanted and none is running, not only on resign-active.
- The Lens in the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen is drawn with SwiftUI shapes (`LensGlyph`), shared with the app's mark, instead of an appex asset. It keeps the teal mark and the gentle pulse, stays still with Reduce Motion, and shows no timer.
- Background connection settings say plainly why it is not running: the toggle is off, location is not allowed, Live Activities are off, or the last start request failed (with its error).
- Threads panel on iPhone: one floating Liquid Glass panel with 30-point continuous corners, inset from the screen edges and full height within the safe area. It is laid out in three zones on that one surface: a 44-point header row (close, title, filter and compose) and the search field, each 16 points from the panel's edges; the list, which fades out where it meets them; and a footer with Settings and Desktop at one height, 16 points from the sides and bottom. Nothing floats over the rows. Rows are transparent; the selection is a soft raised glass capsule (no teal fill) with a semibold title. While the panel is open the chat behind it is dimmed and blurred and its toolbar and composer step away, so the panel is the only glass on screen. iPad's sidebar uses the same zones.
- Thread rows: friendly model names (`deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-free` shows as "DeepSeek V4.1 Flash"), no trailing separator, a time instead of repeating "Yesterday". Generated titles skip greetings, retitle from the first real request, and get a number when they would repeat. The thread count line is gone; Jump to group lives in the filter menu.
- Chat: a hard scroll edge under the title bar so text does not show through it. Jump to latest is a 44-point arrow-only glass circle above the composer that exists only while you are scrolled away. Work in progress no longer expands or collapses on its own as a reply streams.
- Streaming: newly arrived text eases from 15% to full opacity over 0.3 s (ease-out), each chunk's tail a little behind its head, so the newest words read as a soft gradient. One `TextRenderer` per block (`StreamFade`) draws settled glyphs untouched and fades only the newest two blocks, so long replies stay cheap and nothing relayouts. Reduce Motion shortens it to a near-instant crossfade. A finished reply that ends its turn gets a copy and share row, which fades in when the reply ends.

- GlassClientKit unit tests: `swift test --package-path apps/shared/GlassClientKit`
- `apps/glass-ios/scripts/test.sh`: Glass unit and UI tests on iPhone 17 (iOS 26.5).
- Release-path Live Activity states and the round 5 upgrade: Release-built `Glass.app` driven by `LiveHostE2ETests.testKeepAliveLiveActivityStates` and `testUpgradeFromRound5EndsTheOldActivity` against the E2E harness on iPhone 17 Pro Max.
- `testStreamingReplyIsFollowedUntilYouScrollThenResumes`, `testFinishedReplyShowsCopyAndShare` and `testThreadsPanelOverBusyChatShots` on iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPad Pro 13-inch, light and dark.
- `scripts/package-unsigned-ipa.sh round7`: arm64, no `_CodeSignature` or `embedded.mobileprovision` in the app or widget, build 17.
