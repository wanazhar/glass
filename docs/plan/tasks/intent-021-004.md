# Objective

Make policy outcomes and future candidate continuity explicit without turning
historical signals into authority.

# Delivered

Successful candidates now carry revision-bound target fingerprints with route,
role, name, region, purpose, and invalidation conditions. Ambiguous results
derive bounded region/role suggestions from actual candidate differences.
Resolution policies distinguish report-only, confirmation-required, allowed,
and rejected outcomes; no policy path silently collapses ambiguity.
